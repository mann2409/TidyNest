Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 11, 2026

1. Data Collection

TidyNest collects information you provide directly, including item names, box descriptions, and photos you take within the app. We also collect basic account information if you choose to sync your data with our cloud service.

Additionally, we collect:

  • Device Identifier: A unique identifier stored securely on your device to enforce usage limits and prevent abuse.
  • Purchase Information: Subscription status and purchase history processed through Apple's In-App Purchase system and RevenueCat.
  • Email Address: Used for account authentication and service communications.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to provide the app's functionality, specifically:

  • Analyzing photos to suggest item names and categories using OpenAI's Vision API.
  • Synchronizing your inventory across devices via encrypted cloud storage.
  • Enforcing fair usage limits (free tier box limits) using device identifiers.
  • Processing subscription payments and managing your Pro membership status.
  • Validating promotional codes for special offers.
  • Improving our AI identification algorithms.

3. Image Data & AI Processing

Photos you take for AI scanning are processed securely by OpenAI's Vision API. Images are sent encrypted over HTTPS and are not stored by OpenAI beyond what's necessary to process your request. We do not sell your image data to third parties.

4. Device Identification for Fair Use

To ensure fair usage of our free tier, we generate and store a unique device identifier on your device. This identifier is used solely to track the number of storage boxes created per device and prevent abuse through multiple account creation. This identifier:

  • Is generated locally on your device using industry-standard cryptographic methods.
  • Cannot be used to identify you personally.
  • Is stored securely in your device's secure storage.
  • Is only used for enforcing usage limits and is not shared with third parties.

5. Payments & Subscriptions

All payments are processed securely through Apple's In-App Purchase system. TidyNest uses RevenueCat to manage subscription status. We do not store your credit card information. Payment data is handled exclusively by Apple according to their privacy policy.

6. Data Storage & Security

Your data is encrypted both in transit (using HTTPS) and at rest. Sensitive information such as item names, notes, and descriptions are encrypted using AES-256 encryption before being stored in our database.

7. Your Rights & Choices

You have the right to:

  • Access and export all your data at any time through the app's export feature.
  • Delete individual items, boxes, or your entire account within the app settings.
  • Cancel your subscription at any time through your Apple ID settings.
  • Request complete deletion of your account data by contacting us.

8. Children's Privacy

TidyNest is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or via email.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or wish to exercise your privacy rights, contact us at manish.sandil@hotmail.com.

11. Australian Privacy Act Compliance

For users in Australia: This app complies with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) under the Privacy Act 1988. Your personal information is handled in accordance with these principles.
