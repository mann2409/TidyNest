Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 3, 2026

1. Data Collection

TidyNest collects information you provide directly, including item names, box descriptions, and photos you take within the app. We also collect basic account information if you choose to sync your data with our cloud service.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to provide the app's functionality, specifically:

  • Analyzing photos to suggest item names and categories.
  • Synchronizing your inventory across devices.
  • Improving our AI identification algorithms.

3. Image Data

Photos you take for AI scanning are processed securely. We do not sell your image data to third parties.

4. Your Choices

You can delete your data at any time by deleting items or your account within the app settings.
